agg_hirek privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

agg_hirek is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Access: it requests Gmail's "read email" and "send email" permissions. It cannot delete or change email.
  • Use: it reads newsletter emails in the owner's account to collect the article links they contain, and sends related-article emails from the owner's account to recipients chosen by the owner.
  • AI processing: the publicly available articles behind those links are summarized by an AI service. Email contents are not sent to it.
  • Storage: the sign-in token and the article list (titles, links, summaries and email dates) are stored only on the owner's computer. No Google user data is stored anywhere else.
  • Sharing: no Google user data is shared with, sold or transferred to anyone.

agg_hirek's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
