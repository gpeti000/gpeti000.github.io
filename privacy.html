BGCI tracker privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

BGCI tracker is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Access: it requests only Gmail's "send email" permission. It cannot read, search or delete email.
  • Use: it sends the monthly BGCI report from the owner's account to recipients chosen by the owner.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No Google user data is stored anywhere else.
  • Sharing: no Google user data is shared with, sold or transferred to anyone.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
